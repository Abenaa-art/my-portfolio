    04 How we’ll work

    From first idea to final files

    1. Step 01

      Brief

      You tell me about your business, audience and goals. We agree on scope, style and deadline.

    2. Step 02

      Concepts

      I research, sketch and present a few clear directions for you to react to.

    3. Step 03

      Refine

      We pick one direction and polish it — type, colour, spacing — until it feels exactly right.

      Deliver

      You receive all final files (PNG, SVG, PDF) and the source files, ready to use or print.
